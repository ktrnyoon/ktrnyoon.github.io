Hi, I'm Hyejin!

I am an M.S. candidate and photonics researcher at KAIST. I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science with a minor in Physics from GIST. My research background spans electronics and optoelectronics (advisor: Prof. Dong-Ho Kang), integrated silicon photonics (advisors: Prof. Sangsik Kim and Dr. Young-Ho Ko), and non-Hermitian nanophotonics. Across these fields, I have developed end-to-end experience in device design, fabrication, numerical modeling, experimental-system development, and optical/electrical characterization. My strength lies in identifying unexpected connections between seemingly distinct fields and translating them into new device concepts and functionalities.

My research goal is to develop photonic–electronic systems with new functionalities enabled by device, material, and fabrication innovation. I am particularly interested in moving beyond optimizing devices on fixed material platforms toward co-engineering device geometry, materials, interfaces, and integration processes for next-generation information and computing technologies.

Research

01

Integrated Photonics

Microcombs · PIC components · Ring resonators · Second-harmonic generation · Nanofabrication

  • Designed and optimized PIC components including adiabatic splitters, Sagnac loops, and bus–ring coupling structures for broadband, low-loss operation.
  • Designed dispersion-engineered ring resonators (Q > 10⁶) for dark-soliton frequency comb generation and broadband SHG-based spectral transfer.
  • Built a PIC measurement platform spanning 1060/1310/1550 nm on-chip and 500 nm free-space optics, including real-time comb power measurement at 5 GHz sampling.
  • Designed foundry-ready GDS layouts and submitted 200 ring-resonator designs across 20 chips via a Ligentec AN350 MPW run.

Additional collaborative projects with industry partners in this area are covered by NDA and are not detailed here.

02

Non-Hermitian Photonics

Exceptional points · Quantum dots · Angle-resolved photoluminescence

  • Designed exceptional-point (EP) grating structures via COMSOL and RCWA simulation at the University of Würzburg.
  • Experimentally demonstrated EPs in InAs/GaAs quantum-dot membranes using ARPL, TRPL, and Fourier-plane imaging under cryogenic conditions.
  • Found that operating near an EP reshapes and enhances spontaneous emission at the single-photon level.

03

Optoelectronics

2D materials · Neuromorphic devices · van der Waals heterostructures · Nanofabrication

  • Proposed a novel Fermi-level modulation scheme for a ReS₂/WSe₂/h-BN van der Waals synapse.
  • Used an O₂ plasma-treated h-BN charge-trapping layer to enable bidirectional photocurrent polarity switching in a single device.
  • Designed and fabricated the devices via electron-beam lithography and photolithography, and performed electrical/optoelectronic characterization.
  • Validated >95% facial recognition accuracy in system-level SNN simulations, published in Advanced Functional Materials as Front Cover.
