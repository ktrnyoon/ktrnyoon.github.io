Hi, I'm Hyejin!

I am an M.S. candidate and photonics researcher at KAIST, working in the Integrated Metaphotonics Lab. I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science from GIST, with a minor in Physics.

My research goal is to develop photonic–electronic hybrid platforms for next-generation AI hardware. I combine the programmability and memory of electronics with the bandwidth and energy efficiency of photonics to explore device functionalities beyond conventional computing systems.
